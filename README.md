# Bio-Digital Twin Platform GTM Strategy

## Overview

This project develops a go-to-market strategy for a Bio-Digital Twin platform, evaluating customer segments, product positioning, monetization opportunities, and growth pathways.

## Objectives

- Assess target customer segments
- Evaluate product-market fit
- Analyze monetization opportunities
- Identify beachhead market strategies
- Develop customer acquisition recommendations

## Areas Covered

- Market Attractiveness Analysis
- Customer Segmentation
- Competitive Benchmarking
- Pricing Strategy
- Go-To-Market Planning

## Methodology

- Secondary Market Research
- Customer Persona Development
- Competitive Landscape Analysis
- Strategic Positioning Framework

## Deliverables

- Segment Prioritization Framework
- GTM Roadmap
- Customer Acquisition Strategy
- Growth Recommendations

## Status

Research and strategic analysis in progress.
